How to take up arms in the alpha

Sleepy Hallows is in early alpha. Access is through our Discord, and you play with our Unreal Engine launcher.

I · Join the Discord

Everything begins in the Discord — that is where access and the download are given.

Join the Discord

II · Take up the launcher

Download the Sleepy Hallows launcher from the Discord and unzip it. Everything is included — nothing else to install.

III · Raise your character

Run the launcher and press Play. Make an account, choose a realm — Albion, Midgard or Hibernia — and create your character. You begin in Fintain.

Use a password you don't use anywhere else. This is an alpha test server.

What you'll need

A PC
Windows, with a reasonably modern graphics card for Unreal Engine 5.
Space
About 15 GB free for the game.
Discord
An account, to get access and keep up with the shard.
